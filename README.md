# AI-Native Company Builder

**A comprehensive prompt system for designing and building AI-native companies that can run lean and scale fast.**

---

## 🚀 What is This?

The AI-Native Company Builder is a **super prompt** that transforms your business idea into a complete, executable company blueprint. It's designed for founders, entrepreneurs, and builders who want to:

- **Design AI-native operations** (not just "AI as a feature")
- **Run lean** with automation-first processes
- **Scale fast** with clear execution steps
- **Ship quickly** with actionable plans

## 📋 What You Get

When you use this prompt, you get **ALL of these deliverables**:

1. **North Star** — Vision, mission, ICP, differentiators, success metrics
2. **Product Strategy** — MVP scope, roadmap, pricing, competitive positioning
3. **AI-Native Architecture** — Agent roles, tooling, data strategy, evaluation
4. **Operating System** — Org design, weekly cadence, SOPs, decision policies
5. **Go-To-Market** — Channel strategy, acquisition loops, sales motion, retention
6. **Financial Model** — Unit economics, 90-day revenue plan, pricing sensitivity
7. **Build Plan** — Week-by-week execution (0-90 days)
8. **Tech Execution Pack** — Repo structure, API list, DB schema, deployment
9. **Automation Stack** — Tools, automations, agent permissions
10. **Risk Register** — Top 10 risks, mitigations, early warning signals

**Plus:**
- 3 ASCII diagrams (system architecture, GTM loop, data/eval loop)
- Day-1 Checklist (ready to execute)
- First 10 Customers Plan (specific tactics)

## 📖 How to Use

### Step 1: Fill in Your Context

Copy the template from [TEMPLATE.md](./TEMPLATE.md) and fill in your specific details:

- Company name
- Industry
- Target customer (ICP)
- Core problem to solve
- Budget, timeline, team size
- Compliance needs
- Tech stack preferences

**Example:**
```
Company: TaskFlow AI
Industry: B2B SaaS - Productivity
ICP: Small digital agencies (5-15 people)
Problem: Task management chaos across Slack/email/Trello
Budget: $12K USD
Timeline: MVP in 6 weeks, launch in 90 days
Team: 2 co-founders (1 tech, 1 biz)
```

### Step 2: Use the Super Prompt

Copy the entire [SUPER_PROMPT.md](./SUPER_PROMPT.md) content along with your filled-in context.

Paste it into:
- **ChatGPT** (GPT-4 recommended)
- **Claude** (Opus or Sonnet)
- **Cursor** (AI code editor)
- Any other AI assistant

### Step 3: Review the Output

The AI will generate a comprehensive blueprint covering all sections A–J.

Review each section and iterate on specific parts as needed.

### Step 4: Execute

Follow the **Day-1 Checklist** and **First 10 Customers Plan** to start building immediately.

## 🎓 Key Principles

1. **AI-Native Operations** — Design for agents + workflows + data, not "AI as a feature"
2. **Action-First Output** — Every section ends with 3-7 actionable next steps
3. **Lightweight Governance** — Security, privacy, reliability, human oversight baked in
4. **Everything Measurable** — KPIs, targets, instrumentation from day one
5. **Reasonable Defaults** — AI makes assumptions when info is missing (not 20 questions)

## 🛠️ Tech Stack (Example)

The prompt adapts to your preferred stack. Example defaults for B2B SaaS:

- **Backend:** FastAPI (Python), Node.js, or Django
- **Frontend:** Next.js, React, or Vue
- **Database:** Postgres
- **Cache:** Redis
- **AI/ML:** OpenAI API, LangChain, vector DB (Pinecone)
- **Infrastructure:** DigitalOcean, AWS, or Vercel
- **Payments:** Stripe
- **Auth:** JWT, OAuth

## 🙋 FAQ

**Q: Do I need technical skills?**  
A: Basic understanding helps, but the blueprint is designed for non-technical founders too. The Tech Execution Pack can be handed to developers.

**Q: Does this work for non-SaaS businesses?**  
A: Yes! E-commerce, marketplaces, agencies, tools — any AI-native business model.

**Q: How long does it take to complete the blueprint?**  
A: 1-2 hours to generate + review. Then you execute over 90 days.

**Q: Can I customize the prompt?**  
A: Absolutely! Modify sections A-J to fit your specific needs.

**Q: What if I don't have a budget?**  
A: The prompt adapts. You'll get recommendations for free tools and bootstrapping strategies.

**Q: Is this just for startups?**  
A: Primarily yes, but it's also useful for corporate innovation teams launching AI-native products.
