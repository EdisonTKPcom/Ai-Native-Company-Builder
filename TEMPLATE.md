## Pro Tips

- **Be specific:** Instead of "SaaS startup", say "B2B SaaS for dental clinics"
- **Be realistic:** Don't say $1M budget if you have $10K
- **Be honest:** Include real constraints in "Must not do" list
- **Be clear on ICP:** The narrower, the better for initial traction
- **Include numbers:** Revenue targets, customer counts, timelines
